# French New Wave Sequence Plan

## Intent

This is a short AI-assisted 3D animation moment for a French music history
documentary. The image is deliberately surreal: the Eiffel Tower becomes a
rocket and launches, as if postwar Parisian music culture has become propulsion.

The style target is 1950s/early-1960s French New Wave adjacent: location-shot
energy, black-and-white contrast, jump cuts, handheld imperfection, visible
grain, and a little absurdity treated with a straight face.

## Historical Anchor

Use Saint-Germain-des-Pres as the first anchor: chanson, jazz cellars, cafes,
Juliette Greco, Boris Vian, and Left Bank postwar culture. Avoid generic
"vintage Paris" decoration unless it supports that context.

## 30-Second Structure

```text
00-04s  Black title card:
        LA TOUR DECIDE DE PARTIR

04-09s  Static 4:3 shot. The Eiffel Tower sits in a stylized Paris plaza.
        Smoke begins to crawl along the ground.

09-13s  Jump cut closer. The engine glow flickers under the tower.
        Subtitle: "Saint-Germain, encore une nuit sans sommeil."

13-22s  Launch. Flame and smoke take over the lower frame while the tower
        rises. Camera framing is imperfect, as if caught by a documentary crew.

22-27s  Jump cut to a higher, slightly late framing. The tower is already
        escaping the shot.

27-30s  Freeze frame or hard cut to black.
        Subtitle: "Elle ne reviendra pas."
```

## First Polished Test Shot

Make one 8-12 second shot before building the full sequence.

Target:

- Frame range: start just before ignition and end once the tower is clearly
  airborne.
- Render: 4:3 black-and-white, high contrast, visible grain.
- Simulation: medium/high-quality smoke and flame, no low-res preview artifacts.
- Setting: Paris/Left Bank-inspired ground and backdrop, not the current field
  and white base plate.

Acceptance:

- Eiffel Tower reads clearly.
- Flame and smoke feel physically convincing.
- The shot feels like a documentary insert, not a raw Blender test.
- The music-history connection is visible through caption, setting, or both.
